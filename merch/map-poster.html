Bangalore's rock mapWhere the city danced, jammed and rocked, 1930s to today

Band-galore city

From the colonial dance halls of the 1930s to the Palace Grounds stadium gigs, Bangalore built its rock scene a few streets at a time.

Bands formed in its schools, jammed in Koshy's and Chin Lung, played the cabarets of MG Road, and filled the city's pubs, parks and festivals.
