# Rockumentary: Storyboard (structured)

This is the pasted storyboard (about 72 slides) regrouped into chapters for the storymap build. Slide numbers are the page numbers visible in the paste. A running **decade timeline (1920 → 2010 / TODAY)** appears on almost every slide, and in the build it becomes the **Year** readout in the top-left panel.

Legend: 🗺️ = map page (live MapLibre with numbered pins) · 🖼️ = image or media page · ⚠️ = fact to check

## Gaps compared with the research notes (optional additions)

The deck jumps from the 1980s to indie and EDM. The research found material that could fill in:
- **1988, Millennium**: India's first metal band (Bangalore).
- **1989, Pecos opens**, and Bangalore becomes "Pub City" (the deck has Pecos, but no date).
- **Palace Grounds concerts, 2001–2011**: Deep Purple, Roger Waters, the Rolling Stones in the rain (2003), Iron Maiden (2007, about 38k fans), Aerosmith, Metallica (2011).
- **Strawberry Fields (NLSIU, 1997)** and **Bangalore Open Air (2012)**, which would fit on slide 65, "Indie rock competitions".
- **The curfew years, 2008–2018**: 11:30 pm closing, licence crackdowns, Take 5 and bFlat close. This could strengthen "The New Live Music" and "Today".
