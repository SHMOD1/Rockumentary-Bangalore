## 2. Suggested chapter arc (for the storymap)

1. **Cantonment Roots (1960s–80s)**: garrison dances, Brigade Road, Lido, Human Bondage, Usha Uthup.
2. **Pub City (1989–late 90s)**: Pecos and the pub circuit, Millennium and the first metal, Rock Street Journal, Freedom Jam, Strawberry Fields, TAAQ and "Bangalore rock".
3. **Palace Grounds Era (2001–2011)**: Deep Purple, Roger Waters, Rolling Stones in the rain, Iron Maiden, Aerosmith, Metallica; Bangalore as India's concert capital (low entertainment tax, easy access).
4. **The Original Wave (2000s)**: Kryptos, Galeej Gurus, Swarathma, Raghu Dixit, Inner Sanctum; covers give way to original music; Hard Rock Cafe opens.
5. **Last Call (2008–2018)**: 11:30 pm deadline, licence crackdowns, Take 5 and bFlat close; musicians leave for other cities; Shillong claims the "rock capital" title.
6. **Encore (2012–today)**: Bangalore Open Air, Kryptos at Wacken, The Humming Tree, IME museum, 1 am weekends, Hard Rock's exit.

**Top-left panel ideas** (replacing "Population"): number of live-music venues open, crowd size of the chapter's gig (for example 38,000 for Iron Maiden), or a running count of international acts hosted.

## 4. Key bands

- **Human Bondage** (c. 1970–76): raga rock; Radha Thomas, Henry Babu Joseph, Ramesh Shotham.
- **Millennium** (1988): India's first metal band.
- **Thermal and a Quarter / TAAQ** (1996): "Bangalore rock"; early Creative Commons releases; opened for Deep Purple (2001) and Guns N' Roses (2012); featured on NPR (2005).
- **Kryptos** (1998): heavy metal; Wacken 2013.
- **Galeej Gurus** (2000): alt/indie rock.
- **Swarathma** (2002): folk rock fusion.
- **Raghu Dixit Project**: folk rock; Glastonbury, *Later… with Jools Holland*.
- **Inner Sanctum** (2006): death/thrash.
- Others in the scene: Eccentric Pendulum, Gutslit, Agam, and Motherjane (from Kochi, but a Strawberry Fields alumnus).

## 5. Key people and organisations

- **Collin Timms**: founder of Pecos.
- **Amit Saigal and Shena Gamat**: Rock Street Journal (1993).
- **Venkat Vardhan / DNA Networks**: promoter of the Palace Grounds era and Rock 'n India; his guitar collection anchors MAP's *Rock City* exhibition.
- **Salman U Syed**: founder of Bangalore Open Air (inspired by Wacken).
- **Siddhartha Patnaik**: Freedom Jam.

## 6. Themes

- **The "Rock Capital of India"**: a title Bangalore held for decades and later lost to Shillong.
- **Why Bangalore**: Cantonment and British-era club culture, English-medium colleges, the IT-boom crowd with spending money, and low entertainment tax that drew international tours.
- **Regulation vs. music**: the 2005 licensing order, the 2008 11:30 pm curfew, the 2018 closures, and the 1 am extension.
- **Covers to originals**: the shift from cover bands to "Bangalore rock".
