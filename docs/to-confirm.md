# Rockumentary: what still needs confirming

Every caption, credit or place on the map that is marked “to confirm”, grouped by chapter and then by map pin. Tick them off as you find the answers and send me the details; I'll update the map.

**How to use it:** for each item, find the photographer, date, venue or source (the Bangalore Rock and Blues group, Bygone Bangalore and the original posters are good places to ask). If a photo can't be credited or licensed, it may be better to swap it out before sharing the site widely.
